An alley downtown has reportedly gone silent. No footsteps echo, no conversations carry, and even the rain seems muffled. Some say it’s the perfect hiding spot; others believe it’s a trap. Whatever’s causing the silence, no one lingers there for long.

Step into the silence if you dare—but tell someone where you’re going.
