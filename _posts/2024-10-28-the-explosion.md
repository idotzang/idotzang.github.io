The explosion at the abandoned Aurora Glass Factory shattered more than its crumbling walls—it rattled the uneasy balance of a forgotten corner of the City. Once a sanctuary for squatters and outcasts, the factory now lies in ashes, its secrets buried—or perhaps unearthed—in the rubble.

Authorities claim sabotage, but whispers on the streets suggest something far stranger. Amid the police investigation, one name keeps surfacing: “Marcus.” No one knows who he is, but his connection to the event has turned rumor into obsession.

Aurora Glass, infamous for its enigmatic history, was said to produce more than windows—objects infused with unspoken significance. Some believe the explosion was no accident but a calculated act to silence the past or release its dark potential.

As investigators tread carefully through the charred remains, one question burns brighter than the flames: who—or what—is Marcus?
