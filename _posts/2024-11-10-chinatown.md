Last night, Chinatown became a stage for something extraordinary—and terrifying. Gunfire crackled through the labyrinth of streets, helicopters roared overhead, and black vehicles prowled like predators. As the chaos unfolded, the river rose without warning, flooding the area in a deluge unseen for decades.

Who pulled the triggers? Who sent the helicopters? And what summoned the flood? Whispers in dimly lit corners tell of shadowy figures locked in battle, their purpose unknown. Some claim it was a vendetta between rival powers; others suggest something deeper—a clash over an object or secret too dangerous to fall into the wrong hands.

The flood’s timing feels too precise to be natural. Did it cleanse something from Chinatown, or was it summoned to seal an unseen conflict? The helicopters have vanished, and the black vehicles are gone, but the questions they leave behind linger like smoke.

What really happened last night?
