title: "The River Dreamt Something Massive"
date:   2025-03-28
---

Before midnight yesterday, the river whispered its own secret. 
A colossal object — no lights, no sound, no flag — moved down the river like it belonged there… or didn’t care it didn’t. 
Witnesses said it wasn’t floating, just moving, like it didn’t touch the river so much as command it.

No radar ping. No alerts. No denial or claim.

“It was watching”, someone told me. That part keeps surfacing. It was watching us.
What was it? an army vessel? a secret project? a ghost ship? some command ship orchestrating the attack on the city?
Or was the whole attack just a decoy for this thing to slip by?

Eyes on the water. The current remembers.
